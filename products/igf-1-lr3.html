IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1) is a synthetic, 83-amino-acid analogue of human IGF-1. By incorporating a Glu-to-Arg substitution at position 3 and a 13-amino-acid extension at the N-terminus, it exhibits dramatically reduced affinity for IGF-binding proteins (IGFBPs). This significantly increases its biological half-life and stability in preclinical research assays compared to standard IGF-1. Supplied as a lyophilised solid for in-vitro research use.
